01 — WHY I FELL

It started unexpectedly.

We began as two people who happened to find each other online. I remember that first feeling: I couldn't stop thinking about you. At first, I didn't even understand why. There was simply something about you that stayed in my mind.

Then I started discovering you — your heart, your mind, your humour, your dreams, your interests, your little ways of thinking, your imperfections, your strengths and your softness. The more I learned, the less it felt like I was falling for an idea of you. I was falling for Sofia herself.

Even when my feelings were difficult to say, I cared about your happiness. I wanted you to be okay. I wanted you to feel valued. And somehow, through conversations, distance, memories, mistakes, growth and all the ordinary little moments between us, you became part of my everyday life.

“I didn't fall in love with a perfect person. I fell in love with a real person — you.”
02 — WHAT I LOVE

Not just what I see.

Yes, I think you're beautiful. The most beautiful, gorgeous, amazing, pretty, cute and unbelievably attractive girl in my world. But that isn't the foundation of why I love you.

I fell in love with what is inside you: your heart, your soul, your character, your weaknesses and your strength, your mind and your intelligence, your thoughtfulness, your kindness, your heart of gold, your love — everything that makes you Sofia.

I don't want a polished version of you. I don't want you to have to become someone else to deserve love. I love the person behind everything: the person with real emotions, real imperfections, real dreams, real fears, real strengths and a heart that is uniquely yours.

03 — WHY YOU MAY FEEL LOVED

Because I choose to value you.

I think one reason our connection became so meaningful is that I try to make it intentional. I notice your feelings. I listen. I try to learn from my mistakes. I want you to feel safe, comfortable and respected with me.

Distance hasn't made you feel smaller in my life. If anything, it taught me how much a person can matter even when they're far away. The letters, the little creations, the memories and the effort are simply different ways of saying the same thing: you matter to me.

04 — THE OVERTHINKER IN ME

My mind doesn't always know how to be quiet.

I need you to know something honestly: I am a real overthinker. You can be on my mind in the morning, at night, in the middle of an ordinary moment, and in the quiet moments when my brain suddenly decides to replay everything.

Sometimes I think about what you meant, what you're feeling, whether you're okay, whether I said something wrong, whether I could have handled something better. My mind can run far ahead of reality.

That doesn't mean I don't trust you. It means my brain sometimes turns caring into a hundred questions. I'm learning to slow those questions down, communicate instead of assuming, and remember that thoughts are not automatically facts.

05 — THE JEALOUS PART OF ME

I'm honest about this too.

I can get jealous very easily. Even something as simple as Yuria talking to you can sometimes trigger that feeling in me. And yes, other people talking to you can do it too.

But I want to make something extremely clear: my jealousy is not a rule for you. I will never use it as an excuse to control you, isolate you, tell you who you can speak to, or act possessive over your life.

Jealousy is a feeling I have to manage — not something you have to obey. You deserve your friendships, your space, your choices and your freedom. I want to trust you, respect you, listen to you and communicate honestly rather than turn a feeling into control.

“I can feel jealous and still choose trust, respect and freedom.”
06 — WHY RUBEN HIT A NERVE

It wasn't really about you.

Ruben triggered something very specific in me. From what I understood at the time, there were messages and behaviour that made me worry about his intentions and about whether you were being treated with the respect you deserved.

I had also heard about some of the things you were struggling with and the way you could doubt your own worth. That made my protective side react strongly. I didn't want someone to make you feel smaller, reduce you to your appearance, or treat your kindness as something to take advantage of.

But I also learned something important: protecting someone doesn't mean owning them. You are your own person. You get to make your own decisions. My job is not to decide your relationships for you. My job is to respect your choices, trust you, communicate with you, and be someone who treats you with care.

07 — WHAT I WANT YOU TO FEEL

More than anything, I want you to be happy.

Respect

Your boundaries, your choices, your friendships, your time and your individuality matter.

Safety

I want our relationship to feel like a place where you can breathe, speak honestly and be yourself.

Listening

I want to understand you instead of simply waiting for my turn to speak.

Care

I want to be considerate of your feelings and remember that your happiness matters too.

You are more than enough. You don't need to prove your worth to me. You don't have to be perfect. You don't have to perform happiness. You just have to be you.

08 — THE SIMPLEST TRUTH

Why I love you.

Because somewhere along the way, knowing you stopped being a small part of my life and became something woven through it. I love the person you are, the person you're becoming, and the little pieces of yourself you let me see.

I don't love you because you are flawless. I love you because you are you. Sofia for who she really is.

And when my overthinking gets loud, when jealousy appears, when distance feels difficult, or when I don't have the perfect words, I want my actions to come back to the same foundation: love without possession, care without control, honesty without pressure, and respect without conditions.
